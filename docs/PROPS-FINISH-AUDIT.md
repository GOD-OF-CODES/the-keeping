## Ranking formula

score = screen time (1 glance … 5 handled/read or in a cutscene) × closeness (1 = > 4 m, 2 = 2–4 m, 3 = 0.4–1.5 m)
× story weight (1 dressing, 2 story-referenced in `src/story`/`src/cutscenes`, 3 player action: `take_*`, `read_*`,
`pull_bell`). Max 45.

## Recommended build order (biggest visible gain per hour)

1. **Quick wins (spec/one-liners, < 1 h):** hog_cleaver → `steel_cleaver`; sewing_shears → polished steel spec; claw_hammer head → steel, handle → hickory spec; `dust`/`dustOnTop` 0.3–0.6 on all furniture/metal specs (X6); re-enable guest_book cover curl.
2. **X2 normals + bevels in `kit.py`** (weighted normals, bevel all boxes, lathe/tube segment floors) → re-export every prop; then delete the `toCreasedNormals` band-aid in `src/world/opening.ts`. Fixes faceting on rows 7, 9, 10, 16, 22, 25–27 at once.
3. **X1 vertex masks** (curvature/AO/handled/dust → `COLOR_0`) + one shared runtime mask path in `bind.ts`. Biggest single realism gain for every prop at 0.4–1.5 m.
4. **Hero handheld/readable pass** (rows 1–9): letter, locket chain links, ledger/guest-book page blocks, ticket, shears, hammer, jerry can, bell pull.
5. **X3 texel density** for wood_furniture_dark / cast_iron / wood_raw_plank (hero 1024, smaller tileMetres).
6. **X4 hardware kit** (hinges, screws, escutcheons, reveals) across furniture rows 16–26.
7. **X6 grime decals** (wax spill, water ring, soot, rust streak) placed at candles, lamps, fixings.

### Fixed in this review (props lane)

3. **Blank guest-book left page.** The page carries the 6 guest names. It was back-face culled because of a double winding flip in `small_items.py`, and has rendered blank since HEAD. Fixed and rebuilt: the props and the 7 dev bakes are ok, and `--check` is ok (Low 26.93/27 MB).

### Ranked remainder (biggest visible problem first)

1. **Wrong materials on the handheld story props (R3, lead-owned `build-layout.mjs`).** The cleaver is `rust` (a salmon slab), and the hammer and shears are `cast_iron`. The bell pull is `rag_rug`, and the jerry cans are a flat salmon red. Their new geometry and masks cannot read until `steel_cleaver`, polished steel, hickory, embroidered-cloth and painted-steel specs are bound, followed by a props rebuild.
2. **Candle-lit dynamic props rendered near-black (the probe-light fix is applied, #68; the wax SSS is still open).** Every probe-lit prop (all `lighting: 'dynamic'` props: the guest book, candles, cleaver, locket and the rest) gets **no direct candle light**. `src/world/level.ts` l.549 builds `probeLights` from the flashlight, the lightning, the runtime spots and the grids. The candle/lamp flicker lights are excluded (header item 7), and the grids are captured from lightmapped surfaces, so they hold only candle *bounce*. The result: the guest book is pure black beside its own candle while the table under it is lit (`scratch/pd-review/c2.jpg`, `d3.jpg` with the book hidden). One-line fix: append `...runtimeLights.flickers.map((f) => f.light)` to the `probeNode` list only, not to `level.probeLights` (characters, arms and cabin keep their sets). **Applied in this review (CONTRACT-CHANGES #68; the lead may revert):** the book is now warm and candle-lit (`f5.jpg`), every view stays at 60 fps, and draws are unchanged. What remains of this item is the wax translucency. Secondary: the wax generator (`misc.ts`) ignores the spec's `sss: 0.6`, so a burning taper has no warm translucent glow at its top 2–3 cm.
3. **Torch hot spot at 0.4–0.8 m blows the finishing to grey-white.** This affects the sawbuck top, guest-book pages and the VACANCY plate. At 0.5 m the beam gives several klux, and exposure and AgX desaturate the hot spot, so the grain, wear and grime are invisible exactly where the player looks. This is the runtime/exposure lane.
4. **Masks are present but too subtle at play distance.** Edge, cavity, handled and dust masks reach the shader (wearView composite verified), but at 1–1.5 m under candlelight the edge wear and grime are barely visible.
