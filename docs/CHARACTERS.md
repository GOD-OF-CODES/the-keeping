# Characters & animation — Ada, Harlan, first-person arms (lane A)

Everything here is generated by our own Blender Python (no downloaded meshes, textures, mocap or fonts).

```
node scripts/assets.mjs --only characters,anims     # full build (2048 textures), ~10 min, serial Blender lock
node blender/characters/dev.mjs <script.py> [args]   # dev runs; takes the same lock as scripts/assets.mjs
node blender/characters/check_chars.mjs              # runtime-side contract check (three r186 GLTFLoader in Node)
node blender/characters/dev.mjs blender/characters/review_opening.py   # C2 tableau review render
```

| Job | Script | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `characters` | `blender/characters/build_characters.py` (`--only ada,harlan,arms --tex 2048 --no-render`) | `.cache/characters/<char>.blend` (rig + skinned meshes + shape keys + materials), textures in `public/assets/<tier>/` |
| `anims` | `blender/anim/build_anims.py` (`--only … --clips a,b --no-render --no-export`) | `public/assets/{medium,max}/{ada,harlan,arms}.glb` (identical), `public/assets/low/<char>.glb` (Low variant), `.cache/anims/<char>_clips.json`, review sheets |

Review renders land in `scratch/characters/` (`<char>_turn.png`, `<char>_detail.png`, `<char>_clips_*.png`,
`arms_view.png`, `opening.png`).

## How the meshes are made

1. **Skeleton** (`characters/skeleton.py`) from per-character proportion tables (Ada 1.62 m slight; Harlan 1.88 m big).
2. **Anatomy as an implicit surface** (`characters/body.py`, `characters/sdf.py`): tapered asymmetric-elliptical limbs
   (front/back/in/out radii per row, Catmull-Rom smoothed), a swept torso, and smooth-unioned muscles and landmarks
   placed *on* the base surface by ray casts: trapezius, deltoids, breasts/pecs, glutes, scapulae, belly (Harlan),
   collarbones, ribs (Ada), sternocleidomastoids, kneecaps, malleoli, Achilles, olecranon, ulna head, hands with
   phalanx round-cones, knuckle pads, MCP knuckles, dorsal tendons, thenar/hypothenar pads, and a feature head
   (brow, sockets, nose, cheekbones, jaw, lips, chin, ears). Ada's neck is carved by the half-severing gash.
3. **Meshing** (`characters/mesher.py`): sparse surface nets (coarse 16 mm pass → 2.5–4 mm refinement in the band) →
   Taubin relax + re-projection → decimation. *The Skin-modifier scaffold path the plan proposed is still in
   `body.py` (`scaffold`, `graph_to_skin_mesh`, `fit_to_sdf`) but projection folded the hull at the palm, shoulders
   and face; meshing the SDF directly is watertight and fold-free, so it is the one used.*
4. **Weights**: bone heat (`parent_set(ARMATURE_AUTO)`) on a watertight decimated proxy of the whole body (core
   bones only), then Data Transfer (`POLYINTERP_NEAREST`) to every visible mesh and garment; generated geometry
   (hair, sack skirt, cleaver, flashlight, apron/gown chains) gets explicit weights. 4 influences, normalised.
5. **Hidden skin is deleted** (with overlap margins): Ada keeps head, neck, hands/wrists, shins and feet; Harlan keeps
   only the forearms between the rolled sleeves and the glove cuffs; the arms rig has no skin at all.
6. **Textures** (`characters/texbake.py` + `tex_ada.py`, `tex_harlan.py`, `tex_arms.py`): one UV atlas per
   character (smart project, texel density normalised by true area × importance, CONVEX pack), Cycles bakes of
   per-texel position/normal/AO (+ a sack "Fabric" UV), numpy material functions (our own Perlin/fBm/ridged/Worley
   in `blender/lib/noise.py`) → albedo, roughness, height; height → tangent normal map through Cycles' Bump
   (MikkTSpace). The STROUD FEED & SEED stencil uses our own stroke font (`tex_harlan.GLYPHS`, stencil bridges).

## Files per tier (`public/assets/<low|medium|max>/`)

| File | Content | Size max / medium / low |
|---|---|---|
| `ada.glb`, `harlan.glb`, `arms.glb` | skinned meshes, one armature, all clips; meshopt; tangents; no images | Max = Medium; **Low**: clips sampled at 15 fps (`export_frame_step` 2, linear), meshes without shape keys decimated to 50 % (Ada's body/hair keep `jaw_open`/`gurgle` at full resolution); same nodes, bones, clip names, materials |
| `<char>_albedo.webp` | RGB = albedo (sRGB), **A = roughness** (linear) | 2048 / 1024 / 512 |
| `<char>_normal.png` | tangent-space normal (OpenGL +Y, MikkTSpace), RGB | 2048 / 1024 / 512 |
| `ada_hair_albedo.webp` | RGB = strand albedo (sRGB), **A = alpha coverage** (roughness = factor 0.25) | 1024×2048 / 512×1024 / 256×512 |
| `ada_hair_normal.png` | per-strand cylindrical normals (tangent space) | as above |

Every character material shares its character's atlas (UVMap = `TEXCOORD_0`). Material **extras →
`material.userData`**: `material_id` (spec id, see open issues), `albedo_tex`, `normal_tex` (base names, add the
extension from the table), `albedo_alpha` (`roughness` or `alpha`), plus flags: `alpha_mode: 'hash'` (Ada's hair:
alpha-hash, alpha-test on Low), `sss` (Ada skin), `eye` + `clearcoat` (Ada's eye: wet cornea), `void` (Harlan's
head under the sack: unlit black), `lens` + `emissive` + `emissive_color` (the separate `arms_flashlight_lens` mesh: reflector cone + bulb inside the
bezel, material `lens_flashlight` — make it emissive when the light is on).
**`baseColorFactor` carries the spec average as a fallback; when you bind the albedo texture set the colour to
white.** The GLB's `roughnessFactor`/`metallicFactor` are exporter defaults, not data: roughness comes only from
the albedo texture's alpha (hair: `material.roughness = 0.25`), metalness from the spec by `material_id`
(flashlight body `steel_flashlight` and cleaver blade `steel_cleaver` metal 1, everything else 0 — the cleaver's
wooden handle is its own mesh `harlan_cleaver_handle` (`wood_furniture_dark`), weighted to the `cleaver` bone like the
blade: hide/reparent BOTH). Material ids: Harlan trousers `trousers_wool`, twine `twine_jute`, FP sleeves
`coat_rain_dark`, Ada's eye `eye_ada` (all in material-spec.json since 2026-09-30).
`doubleSided` is set for the gown, hair, sack and apron. The sack also has a `TEXCOORD_1` (fabric coordinates) —
unused at runtime.

## Skeletons

Blender: Z up, characters face −Y, their left is +X (glTF/three: they face +Z). **Every bone's local +Z is its
flexion direction, so +X rotation = flexion** (spine/neck nod forward, hip flexion, knee bend, dorsiflexion, elbow
bend, finger curl toward the palm, clavicle shrug, jaw open). Rest = A-pose (arms 42° down).

**Shared core (55)**: `root` (non-deform, at the feet), `hips`, `spine_01..03`, `neck_01`, `neck_02`, `head`, `jaw`,
`clavicle/upperarm/forearm/hand_{l,r}`, `{thumb,index,middle,ring,pinky}_01..03_{l,r}`, `thigh/calf/foot/toe_{l,r}`.

| Rig | Bones | Extra bones |
|---|---|---|
| Ada | 112 (110 deform) | `jaw_hold` (non-deform, under her chin: IK target for the hand that lifts her head), `hair_<g>_01..04` (8 groups around the head, 32), `gown_<k>_01..03` (8 around the skirt, 24; k = 0 front, counter-clockwise seen from above) |
| Harlan | 64 (63 deform) | `cleaver` (child of `hand_r`; the hog cleaver is weighted 100 % to it — reparent/hide it), `sack_0..3` (skirt of the sack below the twine), `apron_{l,r}_01..02` |
| Arms | 39 (37 deform) | `root` = the camera (eye); `upperarm/forearm/hand_{l,r}` + fingers; `flashlight` (child of `hand_l`), `flashlight_beam` (non-deform, at the lens, +Y along the beam: attach the SpotLight here) |

**Runtime overrides** (clips key them lightly so the GLB looks right without runtime physics):
- Ada `neck_02`/`head`: spring joint (head loll) — clips key a plausible hang; add the spring on top.
- `hair_*`, `sack_*`, `apron_*`: clips bake a gravity damped-track (85 %/60 %/50 %) — replace with verlet chains.
- `gown_*`: clips key a follow-the-thighs motion (front panels ride the knee, back panels the heel); a verlet chain
  colliding with thigh/calf capsules can replace it. Without it the hem clips on the largest chase strides.
- The plastered hair (scalp + face veil) is weighted 100 % to `head`, so the face stays covered when the head lolls
  or is lifted. Only the lengths below the jaw/nape move on the chains.

**Shape keys** (morph targets, `export_apply=False`): `ada_body` and `ada_hair` both have `jaw_open` and `gurgle`
(drive both meshes together: the veil over her face moves with the jaw; `gurgle` parts the gash lips and swells the
throat).

## First-person arms conventions

- Place the `arms` root at the camera, same orientation; the camera looks along the rig's +Y (three −Z).
- The rest/idle pose holds the flashlight in the **left** hand, overhand grip, thumb toward the lens, beam forward
  (`flash_dir` (0.04, 1, −0.05)); the right hand is free, low right. Hide `arms_flashlight` for `arms_wheel`/`arms_key`.
- Interaction clips assume, in camera space (m): door plane ~0.55 ahead (knock at (0.08, 0.43, −0.10)), bell knob at
  (0.16, 0.44, −0.06) pulled 0.17 down, door handle at (0.13, 0.42, −0.36), ignition at (0.24, 0.36, −0.42), wheel
  centre (0, 0.43, −0.30). Use CCDIK on `hand_r` for other geometry.

## The opening (C2) staging

Authored on one timeline (`anim/clips_ada.py`): Ada's root at the origin facing −Y, bent across the sawbuck table
(top z = 0.76, spanning y −0.03…−0.63, long axis along X), head over the far edge. **Harlan's root at
(0.02, −1.08, 0) in Ada's frame, yawed 180°** (he faces her head). **The player is expected on Ada's left (+X),
3–4 m away** — that is Harlan's right. Play `ada_opening` and `harlan_opening` together from t = 0 (15 s): his
left hand follows her hair using her baked head trajectory (`.cache/anims/ada_opening_head.json`). Cues: head
lifted/turned 4.5–7.5 s; her eye opens 9.0 s; the sack turns to the doorway 8–9 s; rope released 12.35 s (voice
"Go on, then." just before). Then `ada_rise`. Review: `scratch/characters/opening.png`.

## Authoring API (for new clips)

`anim/poses.py`: poses are dicts `bone -> (rx, ry, rz)` degrees in the convention above plus `'_hips'` (world
offset). Semantic helpers (`arm`, `leg`, `spine`, `neck`, `fingers`, `claw`, `both`, `mirror`, `track`, `wobble`)
apply the side sign for twist/side-bend. `anim/gait.py`: in-place gait with analytic 2-bone leg IK (walk,
stutter, run, stairs) and `gown_follow`. `anim/clip.py`: `Clip(name, seconds, fn(t) -> pose, loop, ik)`; `ik` maps
a tip bone to `{'bone': helper}` or `{'target': fn(t) -> xyz}` (+ `weight`, `chain`, `pole`, `rot`), solved by
Blender IK and baked with `nla.bake(visual_keying=True, clear_constraints=True)`; each clip ends up on its own NLA
track; stray actions are pruned; export `export_animation_mode='ACTIONS'`, `export_anim_single_armature=False`,
`export_anim_slide_to_zero=True`, fps 30.

  hair chains flaring (gravity bake), the gown reading as bare skin (looser shell, relief, colour separation).
